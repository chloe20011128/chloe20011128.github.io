Meshy / Event marketing

Meshy at GDC.

I planned and delivered Meshy’s three-day expo booth at the 2025 Game Developers Conference in San Francisco.

See the booth

The challenge

Design a booth where visitors could see examples of Meshy’s 3D models and try the product during a busy gaming trade show.

My contribution

I designed the booth layout and model displays, and planned how visitors would move from seeing examples to trying the product. During my internship, I also helped shape the messaging for new model previews on Meshy’s own channels.

Role
Marketing Intern, Meshy
Expo dates
19–21 March 2025
Venue
Moscone Center, San Francisco

From render to show floor

What visitors could try

Meshy’s expo programme included guided demos on 3D concept art, character creation and texture exploration, plus a hands-on creator challenge. One demo showed how to refine a Meshy asset in Blender and bring it into Unity for prototyping.
