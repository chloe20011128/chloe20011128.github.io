For You Gift Store / Seasonal retail

Valentine’s Day Flower Menu.

A seasonal menu that helps customers compare bouquet styles, colours and prices before placing a Valentine’s Day preorder.

View selected pages

The brief

For You Gift Store’s Valentine’s Day range included multiple bouquet styles, stem counts and colour options. The menu needed to show what each bouquet looked like and make prices easy to find.

Inside the menu

The cover features a signature red rose and orchid bouquet. Inside, a colour reference page leads into style-by-style pricing and product photography, with a red rose summary and a separate page for preserved flowers.
