RMIT × For You Gift Store / Product catalogue

RMIT Apparel Catalogue.

A product guide that brings garment photography, fabric specifications and branding options together in one place.

View selected pages

The brief

The 2025 catalogue presents customisable garments, from polos and hoodies to vests and outerwear. Readers need enough detail to compare fabric, fit, features and colours across the range.

Inside the catalogue

Product pages use a consistent layout for images, specifications and colour options. Logo mockups show how branded apparel could look, alongside finishing options such as embroidery, printing and foil stamping.
